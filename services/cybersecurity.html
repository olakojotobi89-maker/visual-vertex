Frequently Asked Questions

Cybersecurity questions

What does a cybersecurity assessment cover?

It can cover agreed systems, applications, processes and controls, depending on the purpose and scope of the engagement.

What is the difference between an assessment and penetration testing?

An assessment reviews security posture and risks, while authorized penetration testing focuses on testing how weaknesses may be used.

Can you help improve existing security controls?

Yes. Security hardening, risk guidance and improvement planning can be included where appropriate.
