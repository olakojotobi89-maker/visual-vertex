Our Approach

Start with the business question

We keep the process focused on producing information people can understand and use.

  1. 01

    Understand the business question

    Clarify what the organization needs to know and who will use the result.

  2. 02

    Collect and organize data

    Identify relevant sources and establish a workable structure for the analysis.

  3. 03

    Clean and prepare data

    Review consistency and prepare the information for the agreed analysis.

  4. 04

    Analyze the data

    Examine the information in relation to the business question and desired outcome.

  5. 05
