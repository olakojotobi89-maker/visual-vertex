Our Consulting Approach

Understand first, then recommend

Consulting should produce a useful direction, not a list of changes disconnected from the organization.

  1. 01

    Understand the organization

    Learn about the goals, people, processes and constraints involved.

  2. 02

    Assess the current environment

    Review existing systems, infrastructure, workflows and technology practices.

  3. 03

    Identify opportunities

    Separate the problems that need attention from improvements that can wait.

  4. 04

    Recommend practical solutions

    Set out options and recommendations that reflect the situation and requirements.

  5. 05

    Create a roadmap

    Organize the work into a clear sequence that the organization can assess.

  6. 06

    Support execution where required

    Provide appropriate technical project support as the plan moves forward.
