Our Approach

Our Approach to AI Projects

We focus on the problem first, then consider whether AI is a suitable and manageable part of the solution.

  1. 01

    Define the use case

    We clarify the task, users, expected outcome and limits of the proposed application.

  2. 02

    Review the information

    We consider the data, content or integrations the solution would need to work with.

  3. 03

    Design the application

    We plan how the AI capability fits into the wider workflow and user experience.

  4. 04

    Test and improve

    We review the result against the agreed purpose and refine the application as needed.
